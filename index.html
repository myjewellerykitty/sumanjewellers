Golden Rose Magic Scheme (GRMS)

  1. For Enrollment in Golden Rose Magic Scheme (GRMS) you must duly fill in & sign the application form and deposit it at the Showroom with two photographs and a copy of any one identity card document (Voter ID / PAN / Driving License / Aadhaar Card / Passport / Credit card with photo ID). Collect the Passbook and keep it in safe custody.
  2. Key Terms:
    • Monthly Installment: Amount to be given by participants to Suman Jewellers every month.
    • Principal Amount: Total installment sum paid by participant across 11 months.
    • Interest Amount: Lump-sum bonus provided upon maturity for non-winners.
    • Winner: Member who wins the lucky draw during the scheme tenure.
  3. In case the passbook is lost, a fee of Rs.50/- will be charged for issuing a duplicate passbook.
  4. The GRMS will start on completion of 100 members and the tenure of the scheme will be 11 months. Every month there will be a draw continuing continuously for 11 months up to maturity. The draw will be held between the 21st and 25th of every calendar month.
  5. Every month one winner will receive Rs.15,000/- (for Rs.8,000/- monthly plan) or Rs.10,000/- (for Rs.5,000/- monthly plan). The amount is added on maturity to the principal amount after 11 months (i.e. one winner every month).
  6. The scheme continues for the winner and the winner remains fully eligible for all subsequent draws up to maturity. Any participant, including previous winners, can win multiple times.
  7. You agree to receive official communications via Phone Call, SMS, or WhatsApp on numbers registered in the Passbook.
  8. You are requested to attend the GRMS Draw in person to inspect that the process is fair and transparent. After the draw is conducted, Suman Jewellers will not be responsible for any objections.
  9. You are requested to inform the showroom in advance about your attendance for the draw by the 18th–20th of each month. Inquire for the exact date, timing, and venue.
  10. Maturity & Interest Rules:
    • Rs.8,000 Scheme (Non-Winners): 11 × Rs.8,000 = Rs.88,000 + Rs.3,000 (Interest) = Rs.91,000/- maturity.
    • Rs.5,000 Scheme (Non-Winners): 11 × Rs.5,000 = Rs.55,000 + Rs.2,000 (Interest) = Rs.57,000/- maturity.
    • Note: The fixed Interest Amount is not applicable if a participant wins a draw; the draw prize replaces interest.
  11. You must pay the first installment by cash or card at the Showroom. Subsequent monthly installments can be made via Cash / Cheque / PDC / Demand Draft / ECS / Online UPI. Always obtain a computerized receipt at the cash counter. Suman Jewellers is not responsible for payments made to unauthorized individuals.
  12. Payments can be made in multiples of the monthly installment. The monthly due date is the 15th of every calendar month. For online deposits (NEFT/ECS/UPI), inform the showroom with the transaction UTR number.
